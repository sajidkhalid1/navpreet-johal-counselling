IT’S OKAY TO HAVE QUESTIONS

A few things you
might be wondering.

What happens in the first consultation?

The free 15-minute conversation gives us time to connect, discuss what you’re looking for, and answer questions about the counselling process.

Can we meet online?

Navpreet offers in-person and online appointments. Please discuss your location and preferred format during your consultation.

Do I need to know what to talk about?

You can begin with whatever feels comfortable. You don’t need to prepare a perfect explanation of how you’re feeling.

How do payment and insurance work?

No payment is collected on this website. Please contact Navpreet to confirm current fees, payment arrangements, and insurance eligibility before a session.
