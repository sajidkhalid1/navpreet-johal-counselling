A PERSON FIRST. A COUNSELLOR, TOO.

Hi, I’m Navpreet.
I’m glad you’re here.

Connection is at the heart of my approach. I believe that understanding our thoughts, feelings, and beliefs can help us find a greater sense of balance.

Whether you’re navigating anxiety, emotional distress, trauma, or life’s changes, we can create space to explore what you’re experiencing and work towards what matters to you.

My role is to offer guidance, tools, and support. Your experience, your voice, and your pace guide the process.

Navpreet Johal

Canadian Certified Counsellor · Master of Arts

HOW WE BEGIN

With curiosity.
With compassion.
With you.

My approach begins with connection and an understanding of your experience. Counselling can offer space to explore the relationship between what you think, feel, and believe.

We can work together towards greater awareness, balance, and the changes that matter to you. There is no need to arrive with all the answers.

Privacy, with care.

Booking collects your name, email, optional phone number, and appointment time so the practice can manage your appointment. Please do not include clinical or sensitive information.
