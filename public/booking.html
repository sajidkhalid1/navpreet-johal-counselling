YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, with care.

Booking collects your name, email, optional phone number, and appointment time so the practice can manage your appointment. Please do not include clinical or sensitive information.

Appointment requests, your selected service, and scheduling data are stored in a protected database hosted in the United States and accessible to the website owner. No payment details are collected. If email delivery is enabled, your email address and appointment details are processed by the email provider to send confirmations and reminders. Essential cookies are used for owner sign-in; there are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Contact Navpreet by phone at (587) 417-5036 for access, correction, or deletion requests. This scheduling tool is not a clinical record system.
